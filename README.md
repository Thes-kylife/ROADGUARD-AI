RoadGuard AI 🚧

AI-Powered Pothole Detection and Road Safety Monitoring System

📌 Project Overview

RoadGuard AI is a smart road safety solution designed to detect potholes and identify dangerous road conditions using artificial intelligence.

The system aims to help drivers identify road hazards, avoid accidents and contribute to safer roads through automated detection, location tracking and community reporting.

❗ Problem Statement

Potholes and damaged roads are major causes of road accidents and vehicle damage. Traditional road inspection methods are often time-consuming and depend heavily on manual reporting.

There is a need for an affordable and intelligent system that can automatically identify road damage and provide timely warnings to road users.

💡 Proposed Solution

RoadGuard AI aims to provide an intelligent solution through:

- AI-Based Detection: Identifies potholes using camera input.
- GPS Tracking: Records the geographical location of detected potholes.
- Severity Classification: Categorises road hazards based on their severity.
- Smart Alerts: Provides warnings to drivers about detected road hazards.
- Interactive Map: Displays previously reported potholes and dangerous road locations.
- Community Reporting: Allows users to report road damage.
- Road Monitoring Dashboard: Tracks reported, detected and repaired potholes.

🤖 AI and Automation

The system aims to go beyond basic pothole detection by introducing intelligent monitoring and automated actions.

A future Road Monitoring Agent could analyse detected road hazards, organise reports, identify frequently reported locations and track unresolved road issues.

🎯 Expected Outcomes

- Improved awareness of dangerous road conditions.
- Faster identification and reporting of potholes.
- Better road safety through timely alerts.
- Community participation in road monitoring.
- A scalable and affordable road safety solution.
